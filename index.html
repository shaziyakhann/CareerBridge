WHAT THE PLATFORM DOES

Ten objectives, one connected workflow

Every module maps to a real placement-cell responsibility — nothing is generic.

01

Manage student career profiles

Skills, CGPA, backlogs, resume status and eligibility in one record.

02

Coordinate internships & placements

Run internship and full-time drives side by side, per department.

03

Monitor recruitment activities

Track every application from submission through to offer letter.

04

Schedule interviews

Coordinate slots across recruiters, panels and student calendars.

05

Recommend opportunities

Skill-match scoring surfaces the roles each student actually qualifies for.

06

Notify students & employers

Status changes, new postings and interview slots trigger instant alerts.

07

Generate placement reports

Department-wise, drive-wise and cycle-wise analytics on demand.

08

Improve employer engagement

A dedicated portal for recruiters to post roles and manage applicants.

09

Increase student employability

Guidance and readiness signals surfaced early, not after a rejection.

10

Support institutional management

Configurable policies keep every drive compliant with placement rules.

WHO IT SERVES

Five stakeholders, one shared record

No more re-entering the same data into five disconnected systems.

Students

Build a profile once, get matched opportunities, track applications and interviews in real time.

Placement Officers

Oversee eligibility, drives and interview schedules with institution-wide analytics.

Employers

Post roles, review applicants, shortlist and schedule interviews from a dedicated recruiter workspace.

Faculty

Advise students using the same eligibility and career-readiness data the placement cell sees.

Alumni

Contribute referrals, mentoring and career guidance back into the same placement network.

CORE MODULES

Everything a placement cycle needs

Six modules covering the full lifecycle, from eligibility to offer letter.

Student Profiles

Centralized academic record, skills, resume status and computed eligibility per drive.

Recruitment Workflows

Configurable stages — applied, shortlisted, interviewed, offered, placed — enforced automatically.

Interview Scheduling

Coordinated slots across recruiter calendars, panels and student availability.

Placement Analytics

Department, drive and term-wise reports with live conversion tracking.

Stakeholder Notifications

Automatic alerts on status changes, new postings and upcoming interviews.

Employer Collaboration

Recruiter portal for posting roles, reviewing applicants and closing drives faster.
